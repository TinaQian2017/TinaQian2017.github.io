### Open-Source Projects

I actively contribute to open-source projects in the AI and data space. One of my recent tools:

[**OpenClaw Chat Mark Plugin**](https://github.com/TinaQian2017/openclaw-dashboard-chat-mark-plugin) — A browser-side plugin for the OpenClaw Control UI that lets you bookmark, star, and mark specific past chat messages, then automatically reinject them as context into future AI conversations. Useful for long-running, multi-turn AI sessions where you need to reference specific earlier exchanges without manual copy-pasting.
